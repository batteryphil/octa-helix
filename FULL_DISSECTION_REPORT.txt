  logic         46351     0.00000  0.00000  0.00000  0.00000  0.00000
  creative      45433     0.00000  0.00000  0.00000  0.00000  0.00000

FINDING — ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED:
  The </think> token ranks between 45,433 and 47,140 out of 50,304 tokens
  — in the BOTTOM 7-10% of the entire vocabulary. Even adding +12.0 to its
  logit produces 0.00000 probability. This means the raw logit for </think>
  is approximately -20 to -25, while top tokens score 0 to +5.

  WHY: The </think> token was added via resize_token_embeddings() with
  random normal init (std=0.02). The LM head row for token 50278 received
  gradient during SFT training BUT the SFT gradient hook FROZE the first
  50,277 rows of the embedding and LM head. The backbone (48 Mamba layers)
  was also frozen. This means the model learned to reason inside <think>
  blocks using embedding representations that cannot route to the output
  token for </think> — the 'close bracket' concept has no path from the
  frozen internal representation to the new randomly-initialized output row.

  TECHNICAL DETAIL: With backbone frozen, the hidden state at the last
  token of a reasoning trace encodes 'more reasoning content' in its SSM
  memory. The LM head projects this hidden state to logits. The </think>
  head row (randomly initialized) has near-zero dot product with the
  'reasoning in progress' hidden state — by chance. No training signal
  was strong enough to overcome this random initialization and the frozen
  backbone cannot reorganize its hidden states to point at this new token.

  REQUIRED FIX:
  1. Warm-start the </think> LM head row: set it = mean of {'.', '\n',
     'done', 'answer', '#'} head rows (tokens that appear near conclusions)
  2. Unfreeze the last 4 backbone layers for 300 steps at LR=1e-6
  3. Train on ultra-short traces where </think> appears at token 5-15
  This gives the backbone a gradient path from 'reasoning concluded' state
  to a non-random </think> head row.
  VERDICT: TERMINATION TOKEN IS STRUCTURALLY BROKEN. FIX IS KNOWN. 🔴
